# v0.1.82

## 更新内容

- 用尽时间预估允许记录缺口；历史尚未覆盖整个时段时，先按已有记录的实际时长计算。
- 相邻记录之间采用线性插值，不修改或补写历史；两次相隔至少 1 分钟的有效记录即可开始估算。
- 不跨越额度补充、重置或超过 5 小时的断档，近期未消耗与重置会先到时仍单独说明。
- 保留紧凑的单行对比，收起时显示实际参考时长，每行也可悬停查看计算依据。

## Changes

- Estimate depletion from available history when full windows or intermediate readings are missing, using the actual elapsed duration.
- Interpolate between readings without modifying stored history. Estimates begin with two valid readings at least one minute apart.
- Keep refill, reset, and offline gaps longer than five hours as boundaries, with separate idle and reset-first states.
- Keep compact single-line comparisons, show the actual lookback when collapsed, and expose the calculation basis on hover.
