# v0.1.81

## 更新内容

- 在额度卡片下方查看预计用尽时刻与剩余时长，默认优先参考最近 1 小时用量。
- 展开可对比最近 30 分钟、1 小时、2 小时和 5 小时的消耗速率，每个时段一行，同时显示预计用尽时刻与剩余时长。
- 基于当前额度周期的连续本机记录计算，不跨越重置、额度补充或长时间断档；近期未消耗、记录不足及重置会先到时单独说明。
- 支持中英文及三种字号，可在布局中隐藏或设为默认展开。

## Changes

- See projected quota depletion times and remaining durations below quota cards, preferring usage from the last hour.
- Compare consumption over the last 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, and 5 hours in compact rows that show both the calendar time and remaining duration.
- Use continuous local records from the current quota cycle without bridging resets, refills, or long gaps. Identify no recent usage, incomplete history, and resets arriving first separately.
- Support both languages and all three text sizes, with visibility and default expansion controls in Layout.
